About Us

Local Style. Personal Attention.

Brittany Nails Salon is a local Carmichael destination for clients who enjoy friendly service, personal nail designs and a comfortable place to slow down for a little self-care.

Clients visit for everything from simple color changes and relaxing pedicures to acrylic sets and detailed designs inspired by photos on their phones. The atmosphere should feel approachable and personal — whether you already know exactly what you want or need help choosing your next look.
